STROKE CARE
Rapid Evaluation & Advanced Stroke Intervention
Certain types of acute stroke caused by blockage of a major brain artery may require urgent intervention.
Where medically indicated, mechanical thrombectomy can be used to remove a blood clot through a catheter-based procedure.
Stroke treatment is time-sensitive. Patients experiencing sudden weakness, facial drooping, difficulty speaking, severe imbalance or other acute neurological symptoms should seek emergency medical attention immediately.

BRAIN ANEURYSM TREATMENT
Brain aneurysms may require monitoring, endovascular treatment or surgery depending on their location, size, morphology and clinical presentation.
Treatment options may include:
Endovascular Coiling
Flow Diversion
Surgical Clipping
The appropriate treatment is determined after detailed neurological and vascular assessment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are recommended to minimise waiting time and ensure adequate consultation time.
What should I bring for my first consultation?
Please carry previous prescriptions, investigation reports, scans, discharge summaries and a list of medications you are currently taking.
Can I get a second opinion?
Yes. Patients seeking a second opinion can bring their previous reports and imaging for evaluation.
Do you provide fertility counselling for couples?
Yes. Fertility evaluation generally considers both partners because reproductive health factors may involve either or both partners.
When should I consult a neurosurgeon for back or neck pain?
Persistent pain, pain radiating into the arms or legs, numbness, weakness or symptoms affecting daily activities may warrant specialist evaluation.
Is every spine problem treated with surgery?
No. Many spine conditions can initially be managed using non-surgical approaches. Surgery is considered when clinically appropriate based on symptoms, neurological findings and imaging.
When is urgent neurological care required?
Sudden weakness, facial drooping, difficulty speaking, loss of consciousness, seizures, severe sudden headache or major head/spine injury may require immediate emergency medical attention.
